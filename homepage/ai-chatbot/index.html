Know before you chat

Bring your own key. Keep control of what you share.

A free OpenRouter account and API key are required to start chatting; the app does not include a key. The chatbot interface says your key stays in the tab’s memory. Free models may be busy, limited or unavailable, so access is not guaranteed.

Your prompts are sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider to generate replies. Avoid sending passwords, personal records, confidential work content or other sensitive information. Review OpenRouter’s and the model provider’s terms before use.

Common questions

AI chatbot FAQ

Do I need an API key?

Yes. Add your own free OpenRouter API key in the chatbot settings. Nandan Pixel does not provide or include a key.

Are all models always free?

The chat lists free-model options, but provider limits, availability and shared quotas can change. A fallback model may be available when one is busy.

Where do my prompts go?

Messages are sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider. Avoid sharing sensitive or confidential information.

Where is the chatbot?

This guide lives on nandanpixel.com. The chatbot itself remains at aichatbot.nandanpixel.com.
