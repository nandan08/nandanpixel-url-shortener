Made for everyday tasks

Straightforward tools, with privacy in mind.

Nandan Pixel brings small, useful browser tools together in one place. Convert text case, generate a password, chat with AI, translate Arabic and English, make a quick random pick, convert a PDF, or shorten a long link, with each tool designed to help you get on with the task.

✓Free tools; AI chat uses free-model options
✓No account for the translator, picker or PDF converter
✓Arabic and English tools for translation and OCR
✓Clear notes on when text goes to an AI provider
✓Password generator uses your browser, not uploads
✓Text case conversion stays in your browser
✓Short-link destinations are stored to keep redirects working

The password generator uses browser cryptography; generated values are not uploaded or saved. Text case conversions, the spinner’s name list, and PDF conversion stay on your device. The spinner does not save names after you close it. Please review complex PDF formatting after conversion. For translation, files are read on your device, and the text is sent to Cloudflare AI and, if enabled, Google Gemini when you press Translate. AI chatbot messages are sent to OpenRouter and the selected model provider; the chatbot requires your own free OpenRouter key and keeps it in the tab’s memory. Free models and quotas may change. Avoid sending confidential or sensitive data. Short-link destinations are stored on Cloudflare to maintain redirects; create links only for destinations you are comfortable sharing.
